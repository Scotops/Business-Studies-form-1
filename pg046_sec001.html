Business Studies for Secondary Schools
Economics
a study of scarcity and its implication on production, distribution, exchange and consumption of goods and services
Enterprise
a for-profit business started and run by an entrepreneur
Finance
management of money which includes activities such as investing, borrowing, lending, budgeting, saving, and forecasting
Industry
number of firms producing similar or closely related products
Insurance
agreement between the insurance company and a person at risk for premium
Marketing
activities that promote the buying or selling of goods and services to consumers
Medium enterprise
a business which has 50 to 99 employees
Organising
a systematic process of coordinating, structuring, integrating goals and activities to ensure that organisation objectives are realised
Planning
the determination of the course of action to be taken by an organisation to achieve its goals
Pricing
process of determining and assigning the value of goods and services in relation to a certain currency
Products
refer to goods and services, also known as outputs
Promotion
refers to marketing activity which informs, reminds, and persuades customers to buy a product through advertising, personal selling and sales promotion
Retailer
a trader who sells goods and services bought from the wholesaler in large quantities and sells to the final consumer in small quantities
Risk
potential loss, harm or negative circumstance arising from various situations, actions or decisions
