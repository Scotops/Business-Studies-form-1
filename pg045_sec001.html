Glossary

Accounting

a process involving recording, analysing, interpreting and reporting financial information pertaining to a business

Assertiveness skills

the entrepreneur’s ability to stand up for their own or other people’s rights in a calm and positive way, without being aggressive

Business club

refers to a platform that is organised along clear leadership structures that provide student with valuable competence based learning experience

Controlling

the process of ensuring the performance of business activities take place in accordance with the standards

Customer retention

ability of a business to keep its potential customers

Competitors

other businesses that offer the same or similar goods and services in the market

Demand

quantity of products consumers are willing to purchase at a certain price in a given period of time

Digital marketing

promotion of products to connect with customers using the internet and other forms of digital communication such as emails, social media and multimedia messages

Directing

the action of leading, influencing, and motivating employees to work towards the predetermined objectives

e-business

the use of internet and other technologies used by businesses to market and sell goods and services to customers

Economies of scale

cost advantages that enterprises obtain due to their scale of operation, and are typically measured by the amount of output produced
